# San Francisco guest candidates — researched 27 Aug 2026

Cross-checked against the Notion Databank (36 California rows, all tagged only
"California" with no city) and against the ~80 headings in the outreach doc.
None of the five below appear in either. Verified by web search; emails NOT found.

## 1. Erik Davis — San Francisco
Author of *The Visionary State: A Journey through California's Spiritual
Landscape*, *High Weirdness*, *TechGnosis*, *Blotter: The Untold Story of an Acid
Medium*. Writes on Burning Man, the human potential movement, psychedelics,
Philip K. Dick. Born during the Summer of Love near SF, Yale, ten years east,
back to SF where he lives now. Route: Marian Goodell / Burning Man HQ.

## 2. James Fadiman — Menlo Park, ~87
"Father of microdosing". Ran legal LSD-and-creativity studies at the
International Foundation for Advanced Study in Menlo Park before the ban. Ken
Kesey's Perry Lane neighbour. Sixty years in one field across legal, criminal
and legitimate. Route: Paul Austin / Third Wave, already in the doc; Fadiman has
been on that show.

## 3. Françoise Bourzat — Bay Area
Thirty years guiding mushroom ceremonies in a Mazatec lineage in Mexico, trained
under an indigenous Mazatec woman. Author of *Consciousness Medicine*, teaches at
CIIS, Hakomi practitioner, MA in Somatic Psychology. Fills two gaps on the
roster: the guide's side rather than the clinical side, and a woman.

## 4. Stanislav Grof — Mill Valley, 94
Origin of holotropic breathwork; the map most of the roster operates on. Alive as
of 2026. Two hours in person at 94 is a real question — one attempt, low
expectation, and if it lands it is the most historically significant guest on the
show.

## 5. A Last Mile graduate — San Quentin, 30 min from SF
Men who learned to code inside San Quentin and now work at Slack, Dropbox, Zoom.
87% employment rate; new Learning Center opened 24 March 2026. Co-founder Chris
Redlitz is a VC who walked in for a short talk in 2010 and never left — he is a
decent guest, a graduate is a better one. Nobody is competing for this booking.
