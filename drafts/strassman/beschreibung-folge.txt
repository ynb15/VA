What if the man who put DMT into sixty people walked away from it and spent the next thirty years translating Genesis?

Rick Strassman gave the first dose of DMT in his University of New Mexico study in November 1990 – the first new human research with psychedelics in the United States in over twenty years, after two years of banging his head against the bureaucracy. Nearly sixty volunteers, around four hundred doses. He wrote DMT: The Spirit Molecule, and the phrase has been stuck to the molecule ever since.

Then he shut the study down himself. He has spent the three decades since translating the book of Genesis.

I drove thirteen hours to Albuquerque for this one and slept in my rental car in the Santa Fe National Forest. I brought him cacao as a gift. He told me he is a coffee man, and then we spent two hours on everything the psychedelic scene does not want to hear from the man who started it…

In this episode we explore:

• How he got the war on drugs itself to pay for five years of DMT research
• What his volunteers actually brought back from DMT: eat better, see Chaco Canyon, get a facelift
• Why the pee breaks on Joe Rogan's show were never pee breaks
• Who the universal religion of "we are all one" actually belongs to
• Almost a thousand pages on Genesis, three decades in, and still hunting a publisher

An unfiltered conversation about DMT, the Hebrew Bible, the religion the psychedelic scene built for itself, and what a lifetime of looking actually turns up.
