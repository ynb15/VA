What if the man who put DMT into sixty people walked away from it and spent the next thirty years translating Genesis?

Rick Strassman gave the first dose of DMT in his University of New Mexico study in November 1990 – the first new human psychedelic research in the United States in over twenty years, after two years against the bureaucracy. Nearly sixty volunteers, around four hundred doses. He wrote DMT: The Spirit Molecule, and the name stuck to the molecule for good.

Then he shut it down himself, and has spent the three decades since translating Genesis.

I drove thirteen hours to Albuquerque and slept in my rental car in the Santa Fe National Forest. I brought him cacao. He told me he is a coffee man, and then we spent two hours on everything the psychedelic scene does not want to hear from the man who started it…

In this episode we explore:

• How he got the war on drugs itself to pay for five years of DMT research
• What his volunteers actually brought back from DMT: eat better, see Chaco Canyon, get a facelift
• Why the pee breaks on Joe Rogan's show were never pee breaks
• Who the universal religion of "we are all one" actually belongs to
• Almost a thousand pages on Genesis, three decades in, and still hunting a publisher

An unfiltered conversation about DMT, the Hebrew Bible, the religion the psychedelic scene built for itself, and what a lifetime of looking actually turns up.
