Rick Strassman gave twenty years to a Zen community. They saved his life. He got married there. Then he published one article and they put him in front of a board of inquisition.

It started with the bowing. To the golden Buddha, to the statues, to each other, to the cushion, to the food, to the shower. At some point he asked why he wasn't bowing to the most high instead, and had no answer.

The last straw was a Buddhist magazine devoting an issue to psychedelics in 1996. Private letters he had written to monks surfaced. The verdict: he was selling the wine of delusion.

Broke and separated, he walked past the Eastern religion shelf in a bookstore and picked up a small book called The Kabbalah of Envy…

15 minutes on why an enlightenment has no words in it, and a prophecy does.
