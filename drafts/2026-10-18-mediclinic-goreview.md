## Text zum Einfügen

Thanks for replying to my Google review and asking me to bring this to you
directly — so here it is, with the detail that didn't fit there.

I came in on Saturday 17 October, three weeks after a motorcycle accident, for
one reason: an MRI of my knee. I said so at reception and again to the doctor.
I already had X-rays, and I already knew the likely problem was a torn
ligament — which X-rays don't show.

What actually happened:

- Nearly 90 minutes of waiting, part at reception, part in the exam room.
- A roughly two-minute examination, for about R 3,500, in which my knee was
  prodded and I was told nothing I didn't already know.
- A recommendation for fresh X-rays — the one scan that cannot show ligament
  or soft-tissue damage, and which I'd already had done.
- Then upstairs for the MRI, where I found out the MRI department doesn't
  run on Saturdays.

That last point is the one I'd ask you to look at properly. The MRI was the
stated reason for my visit from the first minute. Someone knew that department
was closed before I paid. One sentence — "we can't scan you today" — and I
would have come back on a weekday. You'd have had the same patient, the same
scan and the same fee, on a day you could actually deliver it.

So my request is simple: a refund of the R 3,500 consultation fee. I'm not
asking you to write off care I received. I'm asking you not to charge me for
the service I came for, which you couldn't provide and knew you couldn't
provide.

And to be fair where it's due: your reception and nursing staff were genuinely
friendly and professional the whole way through, and I said so in the review.
This isn't about the people. It's about a paid consultation for a scan the
building couldn't do that day.

Happy to send the invoice and reference number on request.

Yannick N. Bernard

  Krankenhaus existiert nicht. Wenn es ein anderer Samstag war, ändere die Zeile.

## Was ich an diesem Text gemacht habe und warum

Der stärkste Hebel ist **nicht** die Zwei-Minuten-Untersuchung und nicht die
Röntgenempfehlung — das sind Geschmacksfragen, über die sich ein Arzt
herausreden kann. Der Hebel ist: **Du hast für eine Leistung bezahlt, die das
Haus an dem Tag nicht erbringen konnte, und das war vorher bekannt.** Darum
steht dieser Punkt allein, bekommt einen eigenen Absatz und die Forderung
hängt direkt daran.

Zwei Dinge aus Deiner Rezension habe ich bewusst **weggelassen**: „tl;dr save
your money and your Saturday" und „a well-staffed way to separate an injured
person from his money". Beides sitzt gut — aber in einer öffentlichen Bewertung
ist Spott Druckmittel, in einem Erstattungsantrag gibt er dem Gegenüber einen
Grund, auf stur zu schalten. Die Rezension steht ja weiter öffentlich; der
Druck bleibt also, auch ohne ihn zu wiederholen.

**Register-Vorbehalt, ehrlich:** „Beschwerde" ist in `voice/` noch nicht
gemessen. Diese Fassung folgt der Rhythmik Deiner eigenen Rezension — kurze
Absätze, harte Zahlen, ein Zugeständnis am Schluss. Sobald Du sie abgeschickt
hast, ziehe ich die Differenz zwischen meiner und Deiner Fassung und lege
`voice/` eine vierte Situation an.
