## Body

"We have never sat on a whiteboard and said, how do we become the most well-known show in
Venice Beach."

Samir said that to Raj Shamani. I sat down with that whiteboard the same night, with Cape Town
written where Venice was.

Colin, Samir - my name is Yannick, host of Man's Search for Meaning, a podcast about humans
who've walked through the fire and came out different. I've been listening for years, and two
of your episodes have changed how I work.

The first was Alex Banayan. I ordered The Third Door the day that episode went up. What stuck
was the last line of his template - that you completely understand if they're too busy, and
that even a one or two line reply would make your day. Every email I've written since has that
line in it, and the people who said yes include my mentor and GetSmarter founder Rob Paddock,
Burning Man CEO Marian Goodell, ex-NPR boss and Clinton lawyer Ken Stern, and my 77-year-old
psychedelic therapist, apartheid activist and techno icon Rob Bennett. None of them knew my
name beforehand.

The second was Raj. I listened to it in one sitting and then went back through it with a
notebook. The part I keep coming back to is his races line - that he only enters the ones he
is the only one running. So that is what I am doing before anything else: Cape Town. The most
well-known podcast coming out of this city, and then outward from here.

It would be an honour to have you both on the show one day - here, if you ever make it down,
or wherever you are.

Dreaming Big,

Yannick N. Bernard
YNB Ventures LLC
yannick-noah.com
