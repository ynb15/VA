So you're looking for someone to carry Seek Discomfort into its next chapter.

Last time I wrote, I asked you to sponsor a podcast. This one's a bigger ask. A
motorcycle accident in Colorado ate most of the autumn in between - I'm back in
Cape Town now, still building Man's Search for Meaning from rehab.

Why me, in five lines:

- 7+ years in marketing - Germany's leading psychedelic therapy company, Sir
  Richard Branson's retreats on his private island, my own 6-figure agency from
  zero
- A podcast I only ever record in person, on five continents so far - Burning
  Man CEO Marian Goodell, ex-Bill Clinton lawyer Ken Stern, NFL star Eben
  Britton
- Years running retreats, workations and masterminds inside ManKindProject, Art
  of Mondays and Founders Running Club
- I wrote my whole Seek Discomfort journey up on Substack - the dropout, the
  one-way ticket, the leap: [LINK]
- Marketing strategist, podcast host, community architect - 3-in-1

And here's where my head is at. These are opening ideas, not a plan - I'd want a
proper look at your numbers and at how you actually work inside before any of it
is worth much:

- **The free layer stays free.** One public challenge a month, same one for
  everyone, different territory each time: a physical one, then approach a
  stranger a day, then thirty minutes of reading a day. Plus local meetups
  around it. That's the brand's heartbeat and it's open to anyone.
- **A paid community, with Leaps out of it** - themed trips, several formats,
  because people want different things from the same brand. The paid part isn't
  about the money, it's the filter: free gets you a crowd, paid gets you people
  who actually turn up. I've been part of building exactly that at Art of
  Mondays, one of the fastest growing communities for young founders - with
  residencies running worldwide, eight to twelve people to a house. And every
  Leap is an episode: the stories of the people already wearing the words, not
  celebrities.
- **Giveaways as the bridge between the two.** Annual memberships, free seats on
  a Leap, given to the people who actually did the challenges. With an audience
  this size that's the cheapest acquisition the brand will ever have, and it
  keeps the paid side something people want in rather than something they're
  sold.

The invitation from the earlier mails stands either way, for whenever our paths
cross on this planet.

Dreaming Big,

Yannick
